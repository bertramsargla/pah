# Harvest Basket Grocery — Delivery & Substitution Policy (Fictional, Hackathon Demo)

- Delivery fee: $5.99 per order, free on orders over $100 before tax.
- Delivery windows: next-day delivery for orders placed before 8pm local time; same-day delivery
  available in some areas for an extra $4.99.
- Minimum order: $35 before tax and delivery fee.
- Substitutions: if an item is out of stock, Harvest Basket will substitute the closest
  equivalent item **unless** the order is flagged "no substitutions" for that item. Substitutions
  always respect any allergy or dietary flags on the account — an item flagged for a tree-nut
  allergy will never be substituted with a tree-nut product, even if the requested item itself
  does not contain tree nuts.
- Allergy flag: accounts can mark specific allergies (e.g. tree nut) which blocks matching
  products from being added or substituted into any order on that account, including by an
  automated agent.
- Returns: perishable groceries (dairy, produce, bread) cannot be returned once delivered;
  contact support within 24 hours for a refund on a damaged or spoiled item. Non-perishable,
  unopened pantry items can be returned within 14 days.
