# Household Profile — The Rivera Household (Demo Shopper), Fictional

- Household of 3: two adults and one child (age 7).
- The child has a diagnosed tree nut allergy. No tree-nut products should ever be added to the
  order. Products made on shared lines with tree nuts should also be avoided, not just products
  containing tree nuts directly.
- One adult prefers gluten-free bread and pasta due to a sensitivity (not celiac, but avoids
  gluten most of the time).
- General household preference: prefer the store brand (Harvest Basket) over name brands when
  the difference is mainly price and not a dietary requirement, to stay budget-conscious. They
  have said "we don't need the fancy brand for everyday basics like milk, eggs, bread, rice or
  pasta."
- Exception: for olive oil, the household has said they're willing to pay more for the
  name-brand single-origin olive oil (Terra Verde) because they use it for a specific recipe and
  notice the quality difference.
- Monthly household grocery budget target: $450. This is a soft target, not a hard cutoff — the
  household wants to be told if a restock order would push them notably over budget for the
  month, not blocked automatically.
- Delivery preference: standing order is typically placed every 2 weeks, delivered to the same
  address on file. No specific delivery window preference has been recorded yet.
